Intermediate & Advanced

Take your existing skills to professional level in 4 months.

4 months

Basic sewing skills

Designed for those who already have basic sewing knowledge and want to advance to professional level. The Upgrade programme skips the foundation level and dives straight into Intermediate and Advanced techniques, including corsetry, bridal wear and professional business skills.

Eligibility

You must meet one of the following to enrol:

  • At least 6 months of tailoring or fashion design training in a professional setting
  • 1 year or more of tailoring or sewing experience
