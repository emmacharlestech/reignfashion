Meet the Founder

Mrs. Onyebuchi Rita Uche

Founder & Creative Director, Reign Fashion Empire

A passionate fashion designer, entrepreneur and fashion academy owner, dedicated to creating beautiful, elegant and exceptional outfits.

Mrs. Uche hails from Owa Alio, Agbor, Delta State, and is a graduate of Enugu State University of Science and Technology (ESUT). She is happily married to Mr. Anthony Uche, and their family is blessed with two children, a boy and a girl.

Driven by her passion for fashion, creativity and excellence, she founded Reign Fashion Empire three years ago. Since then, the brand has grown from strength to strength, with a vision of reaching greater heights in the fashion industry.

As founder and creative director, she personally oversees every part of the business and runs the Reign Fashion Academy, where she trains aspiring designers to develop their skills and pursue their dreams. Beyond creating beautiful outfits, she is committed to building a reputable fashion brand and making a lasting impact in the fashion industry.

With determination, creativity and faith in God, Mrs. Onyebuchi Rita Uche remains committed to growing Reign Fashion Empire from glory to glory.

Questions, Answered

Ask Us Directly
How long does a custom gown or outfit take?

It depends on the design, the fabric and the number of fittings needed. We agree a timeline with you at your consultation. For weddings and special events, we recommend getting in touch as early as possible.

Can you make a design from a picture I like?

Yes. Attach your inspiration pictures to your enquiry and we'll discuss recreating the look in your choice of fabric, colours and measurements.

Do I need to come in for fittings?

Fitting arrangements are agreed during your consultation at our studio in Asaba. If you live outside Asaba, mention it in your enquiry and we'll discuss what's possible.

How do pricing and payment work?

Every piece is made to order, so pricing depends on your design and fabric. Prices, deposits and payment arrangements are agreed with you during your consultation, before any work begins.

Can you make Aso-Ebi for a whole group?

Yes. We coordinate Aso-Ebi for celebrants, families and guests. Tell us the occasion, the date and roughly how many outfits you need in your enquiry.

Should I bring my own fabric?

Let us know in your enquiry whether you already have fabric or would like guidance on choosing one, and we'll advise you during your consultation.

How do I join the Reign Fashion Academy?

Send an enquiry and choose Fashion Academy (Training) as the service. Training is offered from beginner to professional mastery level, and we'll share course details, fees and the next intake with you.
