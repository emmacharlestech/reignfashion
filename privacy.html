Your privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

This page explains what information Reign Fashion Empire collects through this website, why we collect it, and the choices you have. We keep it short and in plain language.

Who we are

Reign Fashion Empire is a fashion design business and academy based at Anni Victor Plaza, Old Anwai Road, opposite Dwell Petrol Filling Station, Asaba, Delta State, Nigeria. You can contact us about anything on this page at reignfashionempire@gmail.com.

What we collect

When you send an enquiry or register for the Reign Fashion Academy through our website, we receive:

  • your name, email address and, if you give it, your phone number;
  • how you would like us to contact you;
  • the service you are interested in, any event date, and the details of your request;
  • any inspiration images you choose to upload;
  • for Academy registrations, your preferred level and class days, and anything you tell us about your experience or goals;
  • the date and time you gave consent, and basic technical details about your browser.

To stop spam, we also record a scrambled code made from your internet address. We do not store the address itself, and the code cannot be used to identify you.

You do not need to give us any information just to browse the website.

How we use it

We use your details only to respond to your enquiry or Academy registration and, if you go ahead, to arrange your consultation, order or training. We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

We rely on your consent, which you give by ticking the box on the enquiry form, and on the steps needed to respond to your request.

Who can see it

Your enquiry is stored securely on our website's hosting server and can only be viewed by the Reign Fashion team through a password-protected area. A copy of each new enquiry may be emailed to our business inbox, which is provided by Google (Gmail).

If you choose to contact us on WhatsApp, or open our location in Google Maps, those services handle your information under their own privacy policies.

Cookies

Our public website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. To display our typefaces, your browser loads fonts from Google Fonts, which means Google receives your internet address in order to deliver them.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiries for as long as we need them to respond to you and to handle any order or training that follows, and then delete them. You can ask us to delete your enquiry sooner at any time.

Your rights

Under Nigeria's data protection law, you can ask us to:

  • tell you what information we hold about you and give you a copy;
  • correct anything that is wrong or incomplete;
  • delete your information;
  • stop using your information, or withdraw your consent at any time.

To make a request, email reignfashionempire@gmail.com. If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, you can also contact the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

Keeping it safe

Our website uses a secure (HTTPS) connection, and enquiries are stored in a protected database that only the Reign Fashion team can access. No website is completely secure, so please avoid sending highly sensitive information, such as bank details, through the enquiry form.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle your information, we will update this page and the date at the top.
